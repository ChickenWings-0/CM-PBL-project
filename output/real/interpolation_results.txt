  Global Lagrange swings wildly near the ends of the day (Runge's phenomenon),
  which is why the local cubic (4 nearest points) is used instead.
  On real data every method has large curve errors: short appliance spikes between
  two hourly readings cannot be recovered by any interpolation method.
